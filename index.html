          About this app

          Preview build, September 2026

          This app holds the hymn texts of the original Trinity Hymnal (1961) so the congregation can follow along on a phone when a book is not at hand. It is maintained by the media team of .

          What is in this build

          Hymn numbers match the printed book. Hymns are being added in batches, so a number that is not here yet will be added later.

          One hymn from the 1961 edition, number 27, is deliberately left out. Its text is still under an active copyright held by its publisher, and reproducing it requires written permission.

          Not an official publication

          This app is not produced by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Great Commission Publications or the Orthodox Presbyterian Church. Trinity Hymnal is their work. The texts reproduced here are ones that have passed into the public domain.

          Reporting a mistake

          If a verse is wrong or a hymn is missing, tell the media team after the service or send a note to the church office. Corrections go out to everyone the next time the app is opened with an internet connection.

          Credits

          • Hymn texts: public domain, checked against the 1961 edition.
          • Typefaces: Source Serif 4 and Libre Franklin, both open licensed.

          Privacy policy

          Last updated 17 September 2026

          Short version: the app does not ask who you are, does not create an account, and does not send anything you do to us or to anyone else.

          What we collect

          Nothing that identifies you. There is no sign up, no email address, no phone number, and no contact list access.

          What stays on your phone

          Three things are kept in your own browser storage on your own device, and are never transmitted:

          • The hymns you save.
          • Your chosen lyric text size.
          • The last hymn you opened.

          You can erase all of it at any time. Use Clear under Settings, or clear the site data for this app in your phone's browser settings.

          Analytics and advertising

          There are none. No analytics script, no tracking pixel, no advertising, no third party cookies.

          What the server sees

          The app files are served from a static host. As with any website, the host records ordinary web server information such as an IP address and the time of the request, for the purpose of delivering the files and guarding against abuse. We do not read, analyse or keep copies of those logs.

          Fonts

          Typefaces are served from Google Fonts, which receives a request from your device when the app first loads. In the installed version the fonts are bundled with the app so that no outside request is made at all.

          Children

          The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

          Questions

          Speak to the media team, or write to the church office.
